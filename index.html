Standard Indian Salary Structure

  • Basic Salary: 40–50% of CTC. Forms the base for PF, HRA, and gratuity calculations.
  • HRA: 40% of basic (non-metro) or 50% of basic (metro cities). Partially tax-exempt.
  • Transport Allowance: ₹1,600/month standard. Exempt from tax up to ₹1,600.
  • Medical Allowance: ₹1,250/month standard. Exempt up to ₹15,000/year.
  • Special Allowance: Balancing component — fully taxable.

Deductions Explained

  • PF (EPF): 12% of basic, max ₹1,800/month. Deducted from employee salary.
  • Professional Tax: State tax, typically ₹200/month (₹2,400/year). Varies by state.
  • TDS: Tax Deducted at Source based on annual income and tax regime chosen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a salary slip important?

A salary slip is required for: home loan / car loan applications (banks ask for 3–6 months payslips), visa applications, background verification for new jobs, income tax filing, and claiming HRA exemption.

How is HRA exemption calculated?

HRA exemption = minimum of: (1) Actual HRA received, (2) 50% of basic (metro) or 40% of basic (non-metro), (3) Rent paid minus 10% of basic. Use our HRA Calculator for exact figures.

Does this salary slip generator store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your salary information is completely private.
