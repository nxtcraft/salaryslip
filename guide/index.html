PF Calculation

Employee PF = 12% of Basic Salary (or Basic + DA). Ceiling: ₹15,000. If Basic exceeds ₹15,000, PF can be calculated on actual basic or capped at ₹1,800/month. Employer matches the employee's 12% contribution — but only 8.33% goes to EPS (pension) and the rest to EPF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a salary slip?

Earnings (Basic, HRA, DA, Special Allowance), Deductions (PF, Professional Tax, TDS), Gross Pay, Total Deductions and Net Pay (take-home), plus company name, employee details and month/year.

How is PF calculated?

Employee PF = 12% of Basic Salary, capped at ₹1,800/month if Basic ≤ ₹15,000. If Basic exceeds ₹15,000, the employer may choose to cap or calculate on actual basic.

Is a generated salary slip legally valid?

A self-generated salary slip is useful for personal reference and drafts. For official purposes (visa, loan), the slip should be on company letterhead and authenticated by HR. Do not present a self-generated slip as an official employer document.
